# Hnry UK

> Hnry is an all-in-one tax automation and accounting service for UK sole traders, freelancers, and contractors. Sole traders get their self-employed income paid into their Hnry Account, and Hnry automatically calculates, deducts, and pays the right amount of tax to HMRC every time they get paid — then files all their returns too. Hnry is fully Making Tax Digital (MTD) compliant.

Hnry was founded in 2017 in New Zealand, and is now the largest accounting service for sole traders in New Zealand and Australia. Hnry expanded into the United Kingdom in 2023. The UK operation is headquartered at 35–41 Folgate St, London E1 6BX. Hnry (UK) Limited is registered in England with company number 14307093. Hnry operates as a registered agent of Prepay Technologies Ltd (an Electronic Money Institution authorised by the FCA under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011, FRN: 900010).

## Service Summary

### What Hnry Does

Hnry replaces the need for both accounting software and a traditional accountant. When a sole trader receives self-employed income into their Hnry Account, Hnry automatically:

- Calculates the correct amount of Income Tax, National Insurance Contributions (NICs), and VAT
- Deducts and pays these taxes directly to HMRC on the customer's behalf
- Files VAT returns and Income Tax Self Assessment (ITSA) returns when due
- Sends quarterly updates of income and expenses to HMRC in line with MTD requirements
- Manages business expenses, including reviewing and processing receipts
- Provides unlimited invoicing, quote generation, and late payment chasing
- Automatically routes any remaining income to the customer's nominated bank account

### Pricing

- **Fee:** 1% +VAT of self-employed income received through the Hnry Account
- **Cap:** Maximum of £600 +VAT per year
- **Model:** Pay-as-you-go only — no monthly subscriptions, no upfront fees, no obligations
- **Hnry fee as a business expense:** Hnry automatically claims its own fee as a business expense on the customer's behalf
- **Price history:** Hnry has never raised its prices since launch

### What's Included

All of the following are included in the 1% fee at no additional cost:

- Automatic tax calculations and payments to HMRC
- VAT return filing (MTD-compliant)
- Income Tax Self Assessment filing (MTD-compliant)
- Quarterly income and expense updates to HMRC
- Unlimited invoices and quotes
- Invoice chasing for late payers
- Expense logging and management (via app and Hnry Business Mastercard)
- Automatic allocations to savings, investments, pensions, and charities
- Business reports (accepted by banks and mortgage brokers for loans/mortgages)
- Unlimited access to a team of local UK sole trader tax experts
- 7 years of secure digital record keeping (as required by HMRC)

### Making Tax Digital (MTD)

Hnry is fully MTD-compliant. MTD for Income Tax (MTD for ITSA) is being rolled out by HMRC in phases:

From 6 April 2026 (now in effect): sole traders with gross self-employed income over £50,000
From 6 April 2027: sole traders earning over £30,000
From 6 April 2028: sole traders earning over £20,000

Hnry keeps digital records, sends quarterly submissions to HMRC, and files your Income Tax Self Assessments (ITSA) automatically — no manual action required from the customer.
